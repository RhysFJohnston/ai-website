InVideo

InVideo AI is a text-to-video generation platform that creates complete videos from user prompts. Users can describe the type of video they want, then InVideo will automatically generate a script, select relevant stock footage, add music, transitions, subtitles etc. This is then compiled into a finished video. It is designed for content creators, marketers, and businesses who need professional videos without advanced editing skills.
