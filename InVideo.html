InVideo AI | InVideo

InVideo AI is a text-to-video generation platform that creates complete videos from user prompts.
Users can describe the type of video they want, and the InVideo automatically generates a script, selects relevant stock footage, adds music, transitions, and subtitles, and compiles everything into a finished video.
It is designed for content creators, marketers, and businesses who need professional videos without advanced editing skills.
