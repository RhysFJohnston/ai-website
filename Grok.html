Grok’s primary advantage as a model is that it has exclusive access to the full Twitter / X data and live updates. Meaning it is constantly fed up-to-date information about what is going on socially as opposed to facts and outdated news articles about trends and such. Grok can quickly analyse millions of tweets as they happen and is capable of succinctly summarising more nuanced, cultural events that have occurred as opposed to other AIs which often struggle here.
