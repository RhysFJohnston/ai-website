Llama | Meta

Llama is an open source model which means that Meta (the company who created it) released its source code to the public. This means that companies and individuals can download and recreate the AI model and run it on their own devices without an internet connection. This model is the best choice for privcacy concerned users and those who want to host an AI locally, as it means that their data cannot be used by AI companies for training models. It also ensures that any sensitive information is not absorbed by any large AI models for training and potentially repeated in the future.
