OpenAI o1

OpenAI’s o1 model uses a processing method that attempts to simulate human thought which essentially means that it talks back and forth to itself before giving back an answer. This extra time for ‘reflection’ and deeper processing means that it can more efficiently break down complex mathematical problems, better analyse scientific data, and solve complex logical problems without making simple errors that other models would. It sacrifices response speed for improved accuracy and deeper reasoning, meaning it is the best choice for complex equations.
