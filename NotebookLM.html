NotebookLM | Google

NotebookLM specialises in providing specific answers by only pulling information from uploaded files and notes, meaning it will not pull random false information from the web or old data storage when asked questions about an important subject. It can also use audio generation to turn notes, PDFs and study materials into a 'two-host podcast' format for listening and can be prompted in this state. It is the best AI model for teaching or to help you properly understand a field of study.
